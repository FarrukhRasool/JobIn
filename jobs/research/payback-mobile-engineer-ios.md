## What they do

VERIFIED (payback.group facts-and-figures, group overview pages). PAYBACK runs Germany's largest multi-partner coalition loyalty programme: consumers collect and redeem points and coupons across a network of unrelated retail and online partners (Aral, dm, EDEKA, Netto, Amazon, OTTO, About You and others) using one card, one app, or PAYBACK PAY at the till. The paying customers are the partner companies, who pay PAYBACK to access its shared customer base, its multichannel marketing (app, email, print, web) and its purchase-behaviour data; consumers get points, coupons and cashback-style rewards for free. This is a data-and-marketing-platform business wearing a rewards-app front end, not a simple cashback app.

Group structure (VERIFIED, payback.group/group): three entities under PAYBACK Group — Loyalty Partner, PAYBACK, and Loyalty Partner Solutions (which runs travel-industry loyalty programmes, e.g. airline/hotel coalitions).
