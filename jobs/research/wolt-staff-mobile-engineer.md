## What they do
Wolt is a consumer delivery platform: food, groceries and retail goods (pharmacy, pet supplies, and per this posting "apparel, beauty, electronics, auto parts") ordered through one app, live in 170+ cities across 25+ countries (VERIFIED, App Store listing). DoorDash acquired Wolt in 2022, and Wolt now sits alongside DoorDash and Deliveroo as one of the group's consumer brands (VERIFIED, press.wolt.com, and stated directly in this posting). A broader claim that Wolt runs 1,500 retail partners and its own Wolt Market grocery stores appeared only in a search summary (SUMMARY, do not use downstream).

## Stage and size
Founded in Helsinki. Acquired by DoorDash, all-stock deal completed 1 June 2022 (VERIFIED, press.wolt.com). At acquisition the combined group operated in 27 countries, 23 under the Wolt brand, and Wolt CEO Miki Kuusi retained oversight of international operations reporting to DoorDash CEO Tony Xu (VERIFIED, same release). No current standalone headcount or revenue figures found (NOT FOUND). 2025 reporting says DoorDash is exiting four countries under its Wolt and Deliveroo brands (Qatar, Singapore, Japan, Uzbekistan) and intends to merge all three platforms onto one tech stack, calling it "a massive and expensive undertaking" (SUMMARY, not fetched, re-verify before use).

## Tech stack
iOS, Android, server-driven UI with "configurable, component-based views", CI/CD, App Store release processes, AI-assisted engineering tooling (VERIFIED, posting text). Wolt's engineering page says the wider stack is "almost... based on open-source software" with public releases, no languages or frameworks named (VERIFIED, careers.wolt.com/en/wolt-engineering).

## Vision and direction
The posting frames Retail as explicitly cross-brand: "apparel, beauty, electronics, auto parts and more" delivered "across Wolt, DoorDash, and Deliveroo platforms" (VERIFIED, posting text). That lines up with DoorDash's reported 2025 push to consolidate its three consumer brands onto shared technology (SUMMARY, re-verify). This role looks like a newer, cross-brand product line being built on shared mobile infrastructure, matching the posting's emphasis on server-driven UI and cross-team "consistency" (INFERRED).

## The role in context

## Location and work model
Berlin or Helsinki, candidate's choice (VERIFIED, posting and mirror). No remote or hybrid policy stated. Wolt is Finnish, now owned by US-based DoorDash, so a Berlin hire is likely into an existing German Wolt/DoorDash entity (INFERRED, not confirmed).

## Culture and engineering practice
Wolt's engineering page describes "a great environment for screwing up, learning from that, growing" and says teams "build at scale, but stay small at heart" (VERIFIED, careers.wolt.com/en/wolt-engineering). It mentions distributed teams and annual Product Weeks and Hackathons crossing Engineering, Design, Product, Data and Security (VERIFIED, same page). The posting separately wants someone who can operate in a "multi-team ownership" structure where "strong technical judgment is needed to create consistency... across teams" (VERIFIED, posting text), a genuine staff-level bar, not boilerplate.
