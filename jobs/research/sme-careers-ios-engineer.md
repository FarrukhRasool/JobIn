## What they do
SuperAnnotate runs two connected things. The core business (superannotate.com) is a data-centric AI platform for annotation workflows and dataset management, "integrated services for building and managing training data" for companies building or fine-tuning AI models (VERIFIED, superannotate.com/careers). SME Careers (sme.careers) is its expert-marketplace arm, matching subject-matter experts to paid, project-based AI-training tasks such as writing prompts and grading model outputs. This posting sits on the SME Careers side, not the product-engineering side.
