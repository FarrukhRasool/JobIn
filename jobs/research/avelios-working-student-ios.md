## What they do

VERIFIED. Avelios Medical builds a **hospital information system**, written from scratch rather than layered onto legacy software. It covers treatment documentation, administrative workflows and a patient portal, and records structured data across the whole patient journey so that data can drive AI-assisted care later.

Their pitch is that existing hospital IT is built around legacy constraints and theirs is built around how hospitals actually work. Modular microservice architecture, so a hospital can adopt it piece by piece or all at once. Data stays in European data centres or on-premises.

Founded 2020 in Munich by Christian Albrecht, Nicolas Jakob and Dr Sebastian Krammer.
