## What they do

Almedia is a Berlin adtech company. Its known consumer product, Freecash.com, is a "reward-based user acquisition" platform: advertisers pay Almedia to acquire users, and Freecash pays those users (gift cards, PayPal) for completing missions inside games, apps and surveys. This posting is for a **separate, unnamed AdTech product** being built from scratch, not for Freecash itself. VERIFIED (almedia.co/careers, job posting).

## Concerns

Apple removed Freecash from the US App Store on 14 April 2026, citing guidelines 3.1.2(a) and 2.3.1 (scam practices, bait-and-switch, misleading marketing), specifically over deceptive TikTok advertising. Google pulled it from Play the next day. REPORTED (TechCrunch, independent investigative reporting, fetched directly). Almedia's CEO disputed the coverage as inaccurate; both stores reinstated the app by mid-June 2026. For a founding iOS hire, this matters directly: the company's only public iOS track record involved an App Store guideline violation serious enough to trigger developer-account-level scrutiny, and this new role is being asked to build a second consumer-facing product for the same company. Worth raising directly at interview rather than assuming it will not recur.

The posting's "70 million users" figure is now stale against Almedia's own June 2026 figure of 80 million; not a red flag by itself, just marketing lagging reality.
