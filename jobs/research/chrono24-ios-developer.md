## What they do
Chrono24 runs a global online marketplace for buying and selling luxury and vintage
watches, new and pre-owned, connecting buyers with verified dealers and private
sellers. It lists over 500,000 watches from brands including Rolex, Omega and
Patek Philippe, and monetises through transaction/listing fees plus buyer-protection
and authentication services. VERIFIED (about.chrono24.com/en/career, App Store listing).
