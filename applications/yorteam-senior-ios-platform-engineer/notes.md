## Voice pass

Every bullet was rewritten once the content was settled. What came out:

- **Abstract filler.** "modernising it for performance and maintainability" said nothing and is the master CV's own packaging, which `experience.md` explicitly says to drop rather than reuse. "refactoring initiatives" became "refactoring work". "halving TestFlight build time for the team" lost the trailing "for the team".
- **Trailing participles.** "shipping the same reusable components into..." and "introducing the framework without stopping delivery" were both the `-ing` pattern that reads as manufactured depth. Rewritten as plain clauses.
- **Telling rather than showing.** "Built a modular architecture by extracting..." announced the label before the fact. Now it moves the app to a modular architecture by naming what actually moved.
- **Passive framing.** "the SDK integrated separately by AOK and BARMER" became "the SDK that AOK and BARMER each integrate on their own terms".
- **Verb monotony.** Five bullets opened with "Built". Now the openings run Own, Built, Halved, Moved, Pulled, Led, Took, Cut, Held, Ran.
- **The xcframework bullet gained its mechanism.** `experience.md` notes the saving came from automating how the frameworks themselves get built rather than tuning a build server, which is what makes it platform work rather than devops. That distinction matters on a platform posting and the bullet now carries it.

**One thing the rewrite got wrong and had to undo.** Tightening dropped three of the posting's own phrases out of the main column entirely: "reusable components", "modular architecture" and "CI/CD". `check-style.py` caught it as a claims failure against `notes.md`. All three were put back inside sentences that still read naturally. Worth remembering that a voice pass can quietly cost ATS matching.
