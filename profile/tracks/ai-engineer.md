# Track: AI Engineer

Use for AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Computer Vision Engineer, Applied Scientist, Robotics Software, LLM or agentic roles.

## Positioning

An AI engineer who ships. The differentiator is not the M.Sc., plenty of candidates have one. It is that the AI work sits on four years of production software discipline, so the models reach users instead of dying in notebooks.

## Summary template

> AI Engineer with an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and four years building production software. Works across applied machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems, with recent focus on agentic AI and LLM integration. Brings production engineering discipline to AI systems: clean architecture, CI/CD, and code that survives contact with real users.

Adjust the last clause to echo the posting's own language.

## Lead projects, in order

1. **Bedtime Story Teller** for anything mentioning LLMs, agents, MCP, RAG, or AI product work. MCP server, local LLM inference, agentic tool selection, plus a native client. This is what the 2026 market is hiring for.
2. **RosBot SLAM** for robotics, computer vision, or autonomy. Deepest systems work you have.
3. **Scene Detect** when the role has an applied or social dimension.
4. **Deep Vision** for pure ML and model comparison roles.
5. **Indoor Navigation** for sensor fusion or mobile AI.

Rule of thumb: if the posting says LLM, agent, or MCP, lead with Bedtime Story Teller. If it says perception, robotics, or SLAM, lead with RosBot. Never lead with both, the second one goes in a single supporting line.

## Skills order

Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV, scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, Hugging Face, LangChain, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, Git, CI/CD

Swift and SwiftUI go last, in one line. They are evidence of engineering range, not the pitch.

## What to emphasise

- Built a full autonomy stack end to end, not one notebook stage
- Concurrency and systems thinking, the background mapping thread against the main control loop
- Debugging depth, the phantom frontier story is a real engineering narrative
- Knows the limits of their own systems and says so

## What to downplay

- The volume of iOS UI component packages. One line, no more.
- Anything suggesting mobile is the centre of gravity.

## The agentic AI claim is backed, so use it

Your profile claims focus on agentic systems, MCP, and LLM automation. `bedtime-story-teller` proves all three: an MCP server with exposed tools, an LLM planning step that selects its own story source, and a deterministic fallback policy when that reasoning fails. Link it every time you make the claim.

The one weakness is presentation, not substance. The iOS half, `StoryTeller`, has an empty README and an Xcode project still named `InteractiveAvatarDemo`, so anyone who opens it learns nothing. Fix that README before applying to LLM roles, it is an hour of work protecting your best asset.
