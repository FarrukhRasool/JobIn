# Constraints

**Read this before scoring any job.** A role that fails a hard constraint scores zero regardless of how good the fit looks.

Visa status and salary expectation are deliberately not tracked. The goal is to land a role, so neither is used to filter or down-rank a posting. Do not reintroduce them, and do not ask about them during scoring.

Postings written in German usually signal a German-speaking workplace even when they say English is fine, so flag those as a risk rather than failing them outright.

## Application strategy: volume over fit

**Stated by Farrukh on 2026-08-01, and it overrides the cautious reading of the rubric.**

> "My main goal is to land a job first, not THE PERFECT JOB in the first place. My chances are higher if I apply as much as possible."

The rubric's own wording already allows for this. The 45 to 59 band reads "Maybe, only if volume is needed". **Volume is needed. Treat that band as apply.**

What this changes when scoring:

- **Do not skip a posting because a better-scoring one exists.** The queue is not a ranking of what to apply to, it is a list of what to apply to in what order.
- **The over-qualification caps still apply to the number, but not to the decision.** A junior posting still caps at 60 and a Werkstudent posting still caps at 45, because the number has to stay comparable across the pipeline. But a capped score is not a reason to skip on its own. Say the cap was applied, say what it means, and let him decide.
- **Score honestly, then recommend broadly.** Do not inflate a dimension to push a total over a band boundary. An inflated score makes the shortlist useless for ordering the day's work, which is the one job it has.
- **Genuine hard gates are unchanged.** German above A2 is still a real gate, and a posting that fails it still scores zero. Applying broadly is not the same as applying to roles he cannot do.

The thing this protects against is the pipeline talking him out of applications. Tailoring is cheap now that the profile sources are built. A posting he does not apply to has a zero percent success rate.

### One role per company at a time

**Decided 2026-08-01.** Volume means many companies, not many roles at one company.

Two applications into the same ATS reach the same recruiter, and applying to a Junior and a Senior
opening at once reads as not knowing his own level. That costs more than the second shot gains.

When a company has several openings that fit, **pick the highest level he can honestly claim** and
apply to that one only. Name the others in `notes.md` so they are still on record if the first goes
nowhere. If a rejection comes back specifically on seniority, a lower opening at the same company
becomes fair game.

First case: Scalable Capital, where a Junior and a (Senior) iOS Engineer req were open at the same
time. **Farrukh chose the Junior**, against the recommendation here, which was the Senior on the
grounds that it asks for "profound working experience" with no years figure and carries no cap,
while the Junior asks for "initial experience in a professional setting" and caps at 60.

His reasoning follows the volume rule above: an easier yes is still a yes. That is his call to make
and the record notes it so the choice is not re-argued. The Senior req stays in
`jobs/inbox/scalable-capital-senior-ios-engineer.md` as a fallback if the Junior goes nowhere.

**The general rule is unchanged: one role per company at a time, highest level he can honestly
claim.** "Highest" is a default, not an obligation. He may choose lower for strategic reasons, and
when he does, record why rather than treating it as an error.

## Track priority

**Equal.** Neither AI Engineer nor iOS Developer is the primary track. Every opportunity is worth the same effort, so do not put a thumb on the scale for either.

What this means when scoring and tailoring:

- Pick the track from **what the posting actually asks for**, never from which sounds more impressive or which he has more of.
- A strong iOS role and a strong AI role scoring the same are equally worth applying to. Do not rank one above the other in a shortlist for track reasons.
- **Open to blended roles: yes.** On-device ML, Core ML, camera and sensor pipelines, robotics with a mobile component. Those still earn the `PRIORITY-both` flag, because the AI plus iOS combination is genuinely rare there rather than merely dual, and it is the one place his profile beats a specialist.

Equal priority is about not favouring a track. It does not remove the blended-role bonus.
