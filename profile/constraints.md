# Constraints

**Read this before scoring any job.** A role that fails a hard constraint scores zero regardless of how good the fit looks.

Visa status and salary expectation are deliberately not tracked. The goal is to land a role, so neither is used to filter or down-rank a posting. Do not reintroduce them, and do not ask about them during scoring.

## Hard constraints

| Constraint | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| German language level | A2 | Confirmed from CV |
| English level | C1, fluent | Confirmed from CV |
| Base location | Bavaria, Germany | Confirmed from CV |

**Location is not a gate.** He is open to roles anywhere in Europe, the USA, Canada and Australia, and will relocate. Do not down-rank a posting for being outside Germany. Only a location outside those regions fails the gate.

German at A2 is the one real gate. A posting that requires German above A2 in the day to day work is a genuine mismatch, not a stretch. Postings written in German usually signal a German-speaking workplace even when they say English is fine, so flag those as a risk rather than failing them outright.

## Track priority

**Equal.** Neither AI Engineer nor iOS Developer is the primary track. Every opportunity is worth the same effort, so do not put a thumb on the scale for either.

What this means when scoring and tailoring:

- Pick the track from **what the posting actually asks for**, never from which sounds more impressive or which he has more of.
- A strong iOS role and a strong AI role scoring the same are equally worth applying to. Do not rank one above the other in a shortlist for track reasons.
- **Open to blended roles: yes.** On-device ML, Core ML, camera and sensor pipelines, robotics with a mobile component. Those still earn the `PRIORITY-both` flag, because the AI plus iOS combination is genuinely rare there rather than merely dual, and it is the one place his profile beats a specialist.

Equal priority is about not favouring a track. It does not remove the blended-role bonus.
